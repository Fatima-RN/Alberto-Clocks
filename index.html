    About Alberto Clocks

    Alberto Watch Company is a full-service watch repair, appraisal and retail store offering the finest selection of luxury timepieces since 1985. We believe a clock is not just a time-telling device, but an heirloom piece of art.

    Our mission: to deliver timeless craftsmanship and quartz accuracy to every home.

    Our technology: accurate quartz movements and scratch-resistant glass, finished by hand.
